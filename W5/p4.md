A) Explain how you obtain and use surface normals

To obtain the surface normals, i simply use the normals specified in the suzanne.obj.
I parse them from the vertex shader to the fragment shader such that they values is interpolated to each pixel (Phong shading), and use them in per pixel in the illumination model.

B) Explain how this relates to the surface smoothness when you are rendering a triangle mesh?

The light changes based on the normals, so the figure becomes more smooth. For example over a sphere, normals pointing directly to the light gets brighter than those away.
