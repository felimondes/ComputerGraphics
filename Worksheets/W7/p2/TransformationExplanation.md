The problem is that the quad should be textured according to the direction vectors comming from the eye location. However, the quad is represented in clip space, and we need the direction vectors in world space. Therefore we have to go back in the pipeline, by first applying the inverse projection matrix and then the inverse view matrix (without the 4 coordinates as we only care about the direction vectors). These are then used when sampling the cubeMap, and will be the final textures of the quad.
